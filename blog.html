Car Care Blog

Practical, evergreen guides to help you understand your car, prevent avoidable wear and make better maintenance decisions.

🔋

Car battery warning signs

Slow cranking, dim lights and repeated jump-starts can point to a battery or charging issue. Learn what to observe and when to test it professionally.

🌧️

Monsoon car care checklist

Check wipers, tyres, lights, demisting, door seals and visible water leaks before heavy rain. Never drive through floodwater of unknown depth.

☀️

Summer car care in India

Monitor coolant temperature, AC performance, tyre condition and battery health. Never open a hot cooling system cap.

🛞

Tyre pressure and uneven wear

Check pressure when tyres are cold using the vehicle placard—not the maximum printed on the tyre—and watch for uneven tread wear.

🛢️

Engine oil: when and how to check

Follow the owner's manual for the correct procedure, oil specification and service interval. Check only on a level surface and avoid hot moving parts.

🧰

Used-car inspection: what matters

Review service history, VIN consistency, warning lights, tyres, body panels, signs of leaks and diagnostic findings before buying.

🚦

Dashboard warning lights explained

Red warnings often require you to stop safely; flashing engine warnings need prompt attention. Consult your owner's manual for the exact symbol.

❄️

Car AC not cooling properly

Cabin filter restriction, condenser airflow, refrigerant leaks and electrical faults can affect cooling. Avoid topping up refrigerant without diagnosis.

🔍

Why a new car still needs PDI

Transport marks, low battery charge, incorrect documents or stored diagnostic faults can be missed during a quick handover. Use a structured checklist.
