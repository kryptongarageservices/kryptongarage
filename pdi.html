What we check

Documents

We match the VIN on your papers with the VIN on the car, and check registration and insurance papers.

Exterior

Panel gaps, glass, tyre tread depth, dents, paint issues, rust and any other damage.

Interior

AC, controls and switches, lights, instrument cluster and warning lights, steering wheel, upholstery and seats.

Under the bonnet

Signs of impact or repaint, coolant, power steering fluid, washer water and automatic transmission fluid, leaks and engine noise.

Brake fluid

Tested with a brake fluid tester to check its condition, because old, moisture-laden fluid weakens braking.

Battery

12V battery voltage, voltage drop while starting, manufacturing date, leaks and the warranty card.

Thermal scan

An infrared camera helps spot abnormal heat in selected components, connections and A/C outlets.

Scan and test drive

OBD scan for fault codes, plus a test drive.

Tools we use during inspection

The right tools help us check beyond what the eye can see. The exact tools depend on the vehicle and the inspection scope.

▣

OBD diagnostic scanner

Reads supported fault codes and live data. Codes are read together with symptoms and checks, never as a diagnosis on their own.

◉

Brake fluid tester

Checks the condition of the brake fluid so contaminated or moisture-laden fluid is caught before delivery.

☀

Infrared thermal camera

Shows temperature differences that the eye cannot see, helping flag abnormal heat in selected components and connections.

◎

Endoscopic camera

A small camera for hard-to-reach cavities and selected areas, to look for moisture, residue, corrosion or damage.

±

Digital multimeter

Used for suitable electrical checks such as battery voltage, continuity and selected circuit measurements.

▤

Paint-thickness gauge

Compares paint thickness across metal panels to flag areas that may have been repainted or repaired.

⌁

Tyre tread-depth gauge

Measures tread depth at selected points and helps identify uneven wear that may point to alignment or suspension issues.

✦

Inspection light and mirror

Helps examine panel edges, under-bonnet areas, visible underbody sections, leaks and corrosion.
