Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) for New and Used Cars

Buying a new car or a used car? Don’t skip an independent inspection! A new car can pick up damage in transport or sit in a dealer yard for months. We inspect it before you take delivery, so problems are fixed by the showroom first.

What we check

Documents

We match the VIN on your papers with the VIN on the car, and check registration and insurance papers.

Exterior

Panel gaps, glass, tyre tread depth, dents, paint issues, rust and any other damage.

Interior

AC, controls and switches, lights, instrument cluster and warning lights, steering wheel, upholstery and seats.

Under the bonnet

Signs of impact or repaint, coolant, power steering fluid, washer water and automatic transmission fluid, leaks and engine noise.

Battery

12V battery voltage, voltage drop while starting, manufacturing date, leaks and the warranty card.

Scan and test drive

OBD scan for fault codes, plus a test drive.

Endoscopic camera check

Endoscopic camera inspection helps us check hard-to-see areas for hidden fluid leaks, internal damage and rust.

Tools we use during inspection

Paint thickness gauge, OBD scanner, tyre tread gauge, multimeter and an endoscope camera to see hidden leaks and rust. You receive a detailed report with photos. We also inspect used cars before you buy them.

Why it matters

On one new Tata Curvv EV, our PDI found airbag damage, many OBD errors, rust and small dents that a buyer can’t normally see. The dealer fixed them, and the customer took delivery after that.

Tools we use during inspection

The right tools help us check beyond what the eye can see. The exact tools used depend on the vehicle and the inspection scope.

▣

OBD diagnostic scanner

Reads supported control-module fault codes and live data. Codes are interpreted with symptoms and checks—not treated as a diagnosis on their own.

◎

Endoscopic camera

A small camera helps view hard-to-reach cavities and selected engine or body areas for signs of moisture, residue, corrosion or damage.

±

Digital multimeter

Used for suitable electrical checks such as battery voltage, continuity and selected circuit measurements.

▤

Paint-thickness gauge

Helps compare paint thickness across compatible metal panels to flag areas that may need closer inspection for repaint or repair.

⌁

Tyre tread-depth gauge

Measures tread depth at selected points and helps identify uneven wear that may indicate alignment, suspension or tyre issues.

✦

Inspection light and mirror

Helps examine panel edges, under-bonnet areas, visible underbody sections, leaks, corrosion and hard-to-see surfaces.

Why buyers choose an independent PDI

Our inspection combines visual checks, diagnostic scanning where supported, a road test where appropriate, document checks and a written report. It helps you make a more informed decision; it cannot guarantee that every hidden or future fault will be found.
