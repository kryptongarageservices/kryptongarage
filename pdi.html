Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI)

Buying a new car? Don’t skip your PDI! A new car can pick up damage in transport or sit in a dealer yard for months. We inspect it before you take delivery, so problems are fixed by the showroom first.

What we check

Documents

We match the VIN on your papers with the VIN on the car, and check registration and insurance papers.

Exterior

Panel gaps, glass, tyre tread depth, dents, paint issues, rust and any other damage.

Interior

AC, controls and switches, lights, instrument cluster and warning lights, steering wheel, upholstery and seats.

Under the bonnet

Signs of impact or repaint, coolant, power steering fluid, washer water and automatic transmission fluid, leaks and engine noise.

Battery

12V battery voltage, voltage drop while starting, manufacturing date, leaks and the warranty card.

Scan and test drive

OBD scan for fault codes, plus a test drive.

Endoscopic camera check

An endoscope camera lets us look inside for hidden leaks and rust that the eye cannot see.

Tools we use

Our inspection is done with proper instruments, not just by eye. Here is what each one tells us.

Paint thickness gauge

Measures the paint layer on every panel. A reading higher than factory paint points to a repaint or body filler, which can mean past accident or transport damage.

OBD scanner

Reads the fault codes stored in the car’s computers (engine, airbag, brakes and more). Faults can be stored even when no warning light is on.

Tyre tread gauge

Measures the depth of the tyre grooves and shows uneven wear, so we can tell a truly new tyre from a worn one.

Multimeter

Measures the 12V battery voltage and the voltage drop while starting, to catch a weak battery before it leaves you stranded.

Endoscope camera

A small camera on a flexible cable that reaches places we cannot see, to find hidden leaks and rust inside.

A/C temperature meter

Shows how cold the A/C really blows, so a weak cooling system is found before delivery.

You receive a detailed report with photos. We also inspect used cars before you buy them.

Why it matters

On one new Tata Curvv EV, our PDI found airbag damage, many OBD errors, rust and small dents that a buyer can’t normally see. The dealer fixed them, and the customer took delivery after that.
