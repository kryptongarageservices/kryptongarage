About Krypton Garage Services

We are two mechanical engineers with a deep passion for cars. Krypton Garage Services began as a startup built on that passion, about 5 to 7 years ago, with one goal: to give customers every car-related solution in one place, available 24/7.

Today we handle mechanical and electrical work, painting and denting, used-car and new-car inspection, OBD scanning, legal vehicle scrapping and driving licence services, and we stock spare parts, engine oil and coolant.

Our Vision

To help you live peacefully by providing services for your vehicle.

Our Mission

To become a leader in providing all car-related services of the best quality at affordable prices, and to grow our presence across India.
